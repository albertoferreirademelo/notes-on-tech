About

“Sweden is home now, and Brazil will always be the other.” ALBERTO FERREIRA DE MELO

Alberto works at the intersection of data, design and public service, with one stubborn focus: making AI transparent, humane and genuinely useful for the people it is supposed to serve.

He writes in English and Swedish — about explainability in public agencies, family budgets built from bank CSVs, January light, and enclosures that finally snap shut. The working rule of this notebook: show process, not polish.

Off hours it is photography, 3D printing and small electronics — usually with a camera in the bag and a coffee within reach.

Familjeadmin

A little less to keep in your head.

Familjeadmin is a family planning application made by Alberto Ferreira de Melo for his household. It runs on the household’s Windows computer and brings calendar events, preparations and tasks into one local overview.

From messages to a plan

With the account owner’s permission, Familjeadmin reads selected Gmail accounts to identify dates, invitations, reply requests and other practical obligations. The owner reviews the resulting information in the local application.

Gmail access is read-only. The app does not send, delete or archive emails, apply Gmail labels, or mark emails as read. Google Calendar is a separate optional connection; calendar changes require approval and a separate execution step.

This website provides information about the application. It does not provide access to the household’s calendar, messages or local application.

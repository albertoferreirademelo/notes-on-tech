Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Familjeadmin is operated by Alberto Ferreira de Melo for his household’s private planning. This policy covers the local application and its optional Google connections.

Information accessed and purpose

When an account owner connects Gmail, Familjeadmin uses read-only access to read email content and metadata, including sender, subject, message identifiers and dates. It uses this information to identify events, deadlines, reply requests, payments and other household tasks, and to preserve source evidence for review.

Google Calendar access is configured separately. It can read connected calendars and, if write access is explicitly enabled, execute approved calendar changes. Connecting Gmail alone does not grant calendar write permission.

Storage and processing

Retained source messages, extracted records, settings and audit history are stored in a local SQLite database on the household computer. Local backups may contain this information. OAuth credentials are stored through Windows Credential Manager, separately from the database.

Messages filtered as irrelevant normally leave a hash receipt rather than a retained copy of their content. Retained messages and extracted records remain until removed locally; disconnecting an account does not automatically erase previously imported records or backups.

Optional language-model interpretation runs through the configured local model service. The current installation does not use a cloud language-model service for this processing.

Sharing and Google API use

Familjeadmin communicates with Google to authorize access and read the selected Google services, and to carry out explicitly approved calendar writes where enabled. This public website does not receive household records or OAuth tokens.

Familjeadmin does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Familjeadmin’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

Account owners can disconnect Gmail in Familjeadmin and revoke authorization through their Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Previously imported local records and backups must be removed separately if they are no longer wanted. Local filtering does not alter the original mailbox.

For privacy questions or help removing local records, Contact Alberto on LinkedIn.

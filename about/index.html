Work

I work at the intersection of data, design and public service. My focus is on making AI transparent, humane and genuinely useful. Sweden is home now, and Brazil will always be the other.

This site

This site is my working notebook. I write in English and Swedish, document side projects and share ideas before they are fully formed. The goal is to show process, not polish — which is also why the drawing on the front page is different on every visit: the page plots itself while you read, and no two visitors see the same artwork.

Off the clock

Photography, 3D printing and small electronics — and more coffee than is strictly advisable. Curiosity connects it all.

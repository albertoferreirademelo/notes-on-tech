About — Om

Alberto Ferreira de Melo works at the intersection of data, design and public service, with one fixation: making AI transparent, humane and genuinely useful. Sweden is home now; Brazil will always be the other. The notes here arrive in English or Swedish — whichever language the thought came in.

Away from the desk it is photography, 3D printing, small electronics, and more coffee than is strictly advisable. The working rule for everything on this site: show process, not polish.
